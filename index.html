Support for the tower defense game

Need help? Email astub15@gmail.com with the map, the wave, and what happened.

How to play

  • Tap an empty tile, then pick a tower to build there. Tap a tower to upgrade or sell it.
  • Tap Start to send the next wave. Enemies follow the road; each one that reaches the end costs hearts. Survive every wave to win the map.
  • Airstrike and Deep Freeze are free. Tap them during a wave; they recharge.
  • A game in progress is saved between waves. Pick it up with Continue on the home screen.

Stars and unlocks

  • ★ win · ★★ win with 10 or more hearts left · ★★★ win without losing a heart.
  • Free maps open as you earn stars on free maps. The last one, Apex, opens with ★★★ on every other free map.
  • New towers open as you survive more waves in total, across all games.

Purchases

  • Extra towers and map packs are one-time purchases in the Shop. Unlock Everything includes all of them and opens every map and tower.
  • New phone or reinstalled the app? Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases.
  • For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem.
