Privacy Policy

Ramparts TD

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Ramparts TD has no accounts, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It collects no personal data. The only thing it sends anywhere is anonymous play stats, which you can turn off.

On your device

Your progress (stars, best results, settings, and a game in progress) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Anonymous play stats

To see which maps are too hard or too easy, the app sends a short record to our own server when you start the app, start a map, finish a map, end an endless run, finish a daily challenge or make a purchase. Each record contains:

WhatExample
What happened, and on which map"map finished", "Meadow"
The resultstars, the wave reached, hearts left, time taken, and how the game was played: number of towers and their levels, gold left, early starts and abilities used, hearts lost and to which enemy
Which item was bought (purchases only)"Unlock Everything"
The app's version and build, and whether it came from TestFlight or the App Store1.0 (202610101200)
A random install ID and a random session ID that the app makes up itselfa random code
The time2026-10-10 19:22 UTC

That is all. The records contain no name, email, Apple ID, contacts, location, advertising identifier or device identifier. The random install ID is not linked to you, your device or any other app; it only lets us tell "one player tried this level five times" from "five players tried it once". Our server does not store IP addresses or any other information about your device or connection.

The stats go to a server we run ourselves, over an encrypted connection that passes through Cloudflare's network on the way, as most web traffic does. They are never sold or shared, and are used only to balance the game's difficulty.

Turning stats off

Open Settings in the app and turn off Share anonymous play stats. From then on the app sends nothing, and any stats it had not sent yet are deleted from your device. If you turn it back on, the app makes a new random install ID, so new stats can't be connected to old ones.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID; the app only asks the App Store which items you own. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included. Play stats can be turned off in Settings.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
