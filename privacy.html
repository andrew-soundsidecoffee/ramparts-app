Privacy Policy

Ramparts TD

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Ramparts TD does not collect, store or share any personal data.

On your device

Your progress (stars, best waves, settings, and a game in progress) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. It is never sent to us.

Nothing sent to us

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID; the app only asks the App Store which items you own. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
